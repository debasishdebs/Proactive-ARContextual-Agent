**Wave 1 (2026-10-15), motion-aware keyframe capture plus image-memory foundation.**
- **Glasses capture:**
  - IMU motion classifier (STILL / HEAD_MOVING / WALKING); a dwell event when the head settles for 1 s or more after moving.
  - Motion-driven camera: open while moving plus a hold H after motion; early start on deceleration; closed when settled, with brief checks every 5 s, or every 15 s after 10 quiet minutes.
  - "Quick look" setting: 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 s, default 1 s. The hold H is 4 / 2 / 1 / 0 s respectively (0 = bursts).
  - Keyframe gate: dHash and pHash against the last 5 keyframes, Hamming distance over 10; blur floor 60; sharpest frame per window; dwell exception.
  - Rate cap (fixK): non-dwell keyframes at least 3 s apart, at least 6 s while moving. A dwell keyframe can follow 0.5 s after the last one. "Capture now" is exempt.
  - Keyframes have a long side of at most 640 px and are never upscaled (the camera gives 480×640). The motion label is taken at sample time.
  - Heat guard (thermal status plus battery temperature; headroom is NaN on these glasses). Work/rest: work 30–90 min, rest 5–20 min, rest at least 15 when work is over 45; default 45/15.
  - Single camera owner. Camera-busy back-off when another app uses the camera.
  - Rolling p95 camera start-up lead. Metadata carried end to end.
  - HUD capture dot "(o)" for 600 ms in full screen and on the icon (setting `captureCue`, default on).
  - Triple double-tap exit: 1st double-tap arms ("Double-tap again to minimise"); the 2nd shows "Minimising… double-tap again to EXIT" for 1.5 s; the 3rd exits completely.
  - The camera LED is never touched (enforced by a source-scan test).
- **Phone intake:**
  - Durable receipt: `.v2` sidecar, file plus directory fsync, ack only when durable, reconcile inbox and archive, split-pair reunion.
  - Room v2 tables `frame_meta`, `frame_state`, `frame_source`, `frame_cluster`, `frame_vector`.
  - KeyframePipeline processes on arrival: claim/compare-and-set, up to 3 photos per VISION_EXTRACT call (dwell first, then newest); a provenance contract (`source_frame_ids` plus a caption per photo); captions saved long-term.
  - CallBudget: 1 token per 10/12/15 s ("AI call pace", default 12 s), burst 2, 1 in 5 reserved for nudges, user calls bypass. NudgeEvaluator: 5 s settle, 30 s gap, configurable.
  - PhonePowerPolicy: background batching, pauses on thermal MODERATE/SEVERE.
  - Backlog shedding: once more than 40 photos are PENDING, older non-dwell ones go to NEAR_DUP with reason `shed_backlog`.
  - Recovery: claims are released at process start; 15-minute claim timeout as a backstop; Doze-safe drain.
  - Diagnostics: backlog by state, keyframes per hour, calls per hour, latency p50/p95, Retry all, "Skipped to keep up", "Rokid closed the app N times".
- **Foundation:**
  - `LanguageModel` / `CallGate` beneath ModelRouter.
  - `EmbeddingService`; Nomic text transform (LayerNorm, then 256, then L2) and Nomic vision on ONNX Runtime 1.31.0 (16 KB aligned).
  - Nomic v1.5 bundle in the on-device catalog (downloaded and SHA-verified on the phone; NOT bound as the embedder).
  - Embedding-space swap keeps the retired space 7 days with Roll back.
- **Rokid co-existence:**
  - The real wear signal comes from the phone SDK's `onGlassWearingStatus`, forwarded as `pc_wear`. The Android proximity sensor is not a wear sensor; it reads a constant 5.0.
  - Guarded auto-reopen after Rokid closes the app: 8 s, then back-off 30 s / 2 min / 5 min / 10 min, at most 6 per hour. It never runs during a call, never full screen over a scene, and only after an abrupt close, never after a wearer exit.
  - A restart after a kill comes back in icon mode. A launcher open by the wearer shows the HUD.
  - Liveness probe while minimised; probe before declaring the app closed; persisted wearer-exit stand-down, cleared by an explicit open.
