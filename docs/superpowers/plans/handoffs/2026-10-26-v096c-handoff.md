## 1. Design

### Message flow
1. **Glasses tap (and every voice mark: a voice mark IS a tap with `listen=true`).** `MarkTap.onTap` records the mark in the outbox as today (`MarkMessage`, idempotent by `(epoch, seq)`), shows the corner check, sends it. New: the mark carries `photo = true` ("this build will try a photo and tell you how it went") and `onTap` launches ONE photo job (never blocks the tap).
2. **Glasses photo job (`MarkPhotoCapture`).** Owns the camera through the existing `CameraOwner` (mutex, waits at most 5 s for a running sampling episode), opens CameraX, holds it open for the setting `markPhotoHoldMs` (default 3000, clamped 2000..4000, a `HudSettings` field synced like the other capture settings), keeps the sharpest usable frame of the hold, encodes it and stores it in the outbound buffer tagged `markRef = "<epoch>:<seq>"`. The frame travels on the normal `pc_frame` channel; `FrameMeta.markRef` (new optional field) names its mark.
3. **Failure.** Any failure (no capture service, camera busy or bind failed, no usable frame, heat STOP, buffer write failed) sends one best-effort `pc_mark_photo` message (`MarkPhotoResult{epoch, seq, reason}`, new additive channel; an old phone ignores it) and shows a short error line on the HUD (`MarkCue`, 2.5 s, one line, replaces the check; never more than one cue per mark). Success shows `photo` (the existing "photo sent" pulse follows when it is delivered). Voice: the glasses have no speaker, so feedback is display only.
4. **Phone, mark arrives.** `MarkRecorder.onMark` inserts the label with `photo = pending` (glasses said `photo=true`), or a final reason when no photo can exist: `no_photo_support` (old glasses, `photo=false`), `retro_no_photo` (retro voice mark), `phone_mark_no_photo` (phone-mic voice mark). It then looks for an already-arrived frame carrying its `markRef` and links it.
5. **Phone, photo arrives.** `FrameMeta.markRef` -> receipt sidecar -> `frame_meta.markRef` (Room v15). In the SAME transaction as the frame commit (`FrameIntake`) the label (and its `:i` intent row) gets `frameId` + `photo = ok`. Either order (mark first or photo first) ends linked. A second photo with the same `markRef` is not linked (one photo per mark).
6. **Phone, failure arrives.** `pc_mark_photo` -> `MarkRecorder.onPhotoResult` sets the label's `photo` to the reason token (only while pending).
7. **Build.** A mark's Moment second is the second of its linked live photo (`canvas_label.frameId`), else its own second. Linked: the Moment is a FRAME Moment (photo + mark; text lineage per slice A, transcript null). Not linked and `pending` within `PHOTO_WAIT_MS` (120 s from receipt): the mark makes no Moment yet. Not linked and a reason (or the wait ran out: `photo_timeout`): the MARK Moment is the explicit FALLBACK, `Moment.errorReason` = the reason (slice A: valid, purge keeps it). A photo that arrives later upgrades the fallback to a FRAME Moment (the mark moves to the photo's second; the stale MARK Moment is removed by the same rebuild).
8. **User-visible.** Activity (marks list) and the Moments list/API show the reason in words ("No photo: camera busy"), Moments rows carry `errorReason`.

### Where the exception lives and how it is bounded (also in the plan's LA7/LA9 rows)
The exception is ONLY in two places and nothing else changes: (a) `MarkPhotoCapture` (glasses) does not consult `GlassesPauses.captureAllowed`, battery saver or `FrameGuard.lowBattery`/dark, and `GlassesRuntime.bufferMarkPhoto` skips the two `captureAllowed` checks of `bufferCaptured`; (b) `FrameAdmission` (phone) does not apply the pause-history drop to a frame that carries a `markRef`. Bounds: one photo per mark `(epoch, seq)` (the photo job starts only from a tap, never from a resend; at most 3 photo jobs in flight, a 4th rapid tap fails fast with `camera_busy`); user-initiated marks only (no automatic path can set `photo`); at most `markPhotoHoldMs` (hard clamp 4 s) of camera time per mark; one camera user at a time (`CameraOwner`); heat STOP still wins (hardware safety); the regular sampling, Fixed capture, the nudge and every pause/battery/quiet-hours rule are untouched; the photo is buffered like any frame (a later pause toggle still clears the buffer: a pause after the mark drops its undelivered photo, the phone then records the fallback `photo_timeout`); on the phone a `markRef` frame still passes the wipe fence, the Moment Forget fences and tombstones, and an undone mark's frame is refused.

### Undo and Forget
- Mark undo / voice undo (`MarkRecorder`): first forgets the mark's linked photo through the existing gated Forget path (`ForgetActions.forgetMomentOutcome`, tombstone, `dropHolding` of the Moment, outbox cleanup; a failure or running export throws so the glasses resend the undo), then deletes the label rows (`deleteLabel`, Moment staled and rebuilt). If the photo has not arrived yet a `canvas_meta` key `mark_undone:<labelId>` is stored and `FrameIntake` refuses that frame (queued JPEG deletion, same as the Forget fence).
- Forget photo / Forget Moment / range Forget: unchanged paths; `deleteLabelsForFrames` already deletes the labels whose `frameId` is the photo, so forgetting the mark's photo removes the mark with it.
- A photo whose retention expired while the mark's `photo = ok`: no Moment for that mark (neither a photo nor a fallback), like a forgotten one.

### Reasons (tokens, never text)
Phone: `no_photo_support`, `retro_no_photo`, `phone_mark_no_photo`, `photo_timeout`. Glasses: `no_capture_service`, `camera_busy`, `camera_bind_failed`, `no_frame`, `heat`, `not_stored`, `cancelled`.

### Gate semantics
A mark during an export/delete: the mark insert is not gated (as today); undo and photo-forget use the existing `DataOpGate.delete` (bounded 30 s wait, then the typed failure, no ack, the glasses resend). The link and the failure update are single short transactions guarded by the existing fences.

## 5. Decisions for the human
1. **A pause after the mark drops its undelivered photo.** The existing pause clears the outbound buffer; the exception does not protect the photo from that (pause policy untouched, as asked). The phone then shows the fallback with `photo_timeout` after 2 min. Say if a mark photo should survive a pause toggle.
2. **Retention expiry removes the mark with its photo.** The existing lineage (`deleteLabelsForFrames`) deletes the labels of an expired photo, so after retention a mark with a photo disappears from Activity instead of becoming a bare mark. Say if marks should outlive their photo (they would then be invalid mark-only Moments).
3. **Marks from before v15 and retro/phone-mic marks** become fallback Moments (`no_photo_support`, `retro_no_photo`, `phone_mark_no_photo`) rather than being dropped. The one-time purge ruling still deletes the 01:50 ones (tombstoned seconds).
4. **Late photo (after 2 min):** upgrades the fallback; the stale fallback Moment of the mark's own second goes at the next cadence rebuild (up to 15 min) unless the frame commit rebuilds it; both can show briefly.
5. **Feedback is display only** (corner cue 2.5 s for errors, 600 ms check, "photo" confirm); the glasses have no speaker/TTS in this build.
6. **Sharpest frame of the hold** is the photo (not the first); its time is that frame's time (up to 3 s after the tap). Heat STOP still refuses; low battery and dark frames do not.
7. **Failure report is best effort** (no durable queue); a lost one becomes `photo_timeout`.
8. Mark photos also go through the extraction pipeline like any photo (they are ordinary frames with a mark link). A mark whose phone pipeline is paused waits in the inbox as every frame does.
9. Hold options in Settings are 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4 s (any value 2-4 s is accepted on the wire).
