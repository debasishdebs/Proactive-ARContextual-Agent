## 3. Watchdog (instrumented; one human decision)
- Evidence: glasses dead 23:01:11-23:22:38 and 23:43:34-23:49:32. The glasses status log shows capture was `running=false reason=paused` from 22:41:15 until the app died (last status 23:01:44). Phone watchdog code (`GlassesWatchdog`, tick 150 s, silent threshold 3 min): `captureActive = hub.activePauses.value.isEmpty()`, so while ANY pause is active the watchdog is `Off` and never reopens the app. That fits gap 1 (21 min, paused the whole time; the app came back at 23:22:38 with `running=true`, i.e. when the pause was gone). Gap 2 (350 s) is the designed latency: 3 min silence + up to 150 s to the next tick, no defect. Other hypotheses not excluded (no phone log existed): phone process suspension (Doze; `delay` ticks are not wakeups), call guard, worn guard, hourly cap.
- Instrumentation (PcLog tag PcWatchdog / PcRelaunch, counts and enum names only): `watchdog tick verdict=<Off|Ok|HubDown|LinkDown|StoppedByYou|Reopening|Waiting|Probing> why=setting_off|capture_inactive|hub_down|stood_down | link=<state> silent_s=N relaunch=<reopened|pending|why> action=reconnect|probe` on every verdict change and every 10th tick; W `watchdog late tick gap_s=N period_s=150` (a gap far over the period proves the phone was suspended); W `watchdog hub down, starting it`; relauncher `relaunch: link A->B`, `relaunch: attempt in N s (step k)`, plus its existing waiting/reopening lines, now in `pca.log` (`GlassesRelauncher` and the Hub wiring moved to PcLog).
- Not fixed on purpose: the paused-means-Off rule is a design choice, not a clear defect. HUMAN DECISION: should the watchdog keep the glasses app alive while paused (change `captureActive` in `HubGraph.kt` to "pipeline on", ignoring pauses)? Pro: a dead app cannot enforce a timed pause expiry or take a resume tap; con: reopens the app all night in quiet hours. Also: shorten SILENT_MS (3 min) / TICK_MS (150 s) if 6 min dead time matters.
- Files: `GlassesWatchdog.kt`, `GlassesRelauncher.kt`, `Hub.kt` (one log line), tests `GlassesWatchdogTest`, `GlassesRelauncherTest`.
- Mutations (caught): late-tick warning disabled; Off cause swapped; relaunch result dropped from the line; relauncher scheduling line disabled.
- Next window: `grep PcWatchdog pca.log` shows, per dead gap, whether the verdict was `Off why=capture_inactive`, `Waiting relaunch=...`, or ticks stopped (late tick).

## 4. Camera startup
- Evidence and root cause: the "70-75% camOpen in the first minute after every start" is a MEASUREMENT ARTEFACT, not real camera time. The first heartbeat of a process is printed at tick 0, ~40 ms after `smart session started` and 2.5 s BEFORE the first `camera fps range` line; its span is ~250 ms, in which the first episode had been "open" 186 ms; per-minute normalisation then printed `camOpen=73% opens1m=236 camOpenMs1m=43937` (236 = 60000/254 ms). Every later heartbeat reads 0-6%.
- Fix (diagnostic only, capture untouched): `Heartbeat` normalises over at least one minute (`MIN_RATE_SPAN_MS = 60_000`), so the first line reads about `camOpen=0% camOpenMs1m=186 opens1m=1`. `tools/battery-run.sh` averaged these fields, so it was biased by one bogus sample per start.
- Not a defect: `camera not ready on the first bind: retrying in 500 ms` is the designed cold-start grace (no frame within `NO_FRAME_MS` = 3 s of the first open; HAL warm-up of ~2.5 s, first keyframe about 1.5 s later); `camera bind failed (1 of 3)` at 23:25 self-healed in 3 s (counter reset at the first frame). Instrumentation added: both lines now end with `cause=open_failed|no_frames open_ms=N`.
- Files: `Heartbeat.kt`, `SamplingSession.kt`, tests `HeartbeatTest`, `SamplingSessionTest`.
- Mutations (caught): floor reverted to 1 ms; cause/open_ms dropped from the cold line.
- NOT done: warm-up of the camera HAL is not attacked (changes capture semantics).
