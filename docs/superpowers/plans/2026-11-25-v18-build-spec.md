## 0. Owner decisions applied, and deviations from PLAN/CH
1. **Card history is FULL in v18.** `DeveloperFlags.placeCardHistory` defaults ON (`O/orchestration/forget/ForgetShadow.kt:58`: `getBoolean(KEY, true)`; a read failure gives false). All CH Forget cascades and privacy rules stay. The code is boring: one guarded writer (`PlaceCardHistory.add`) and one reader (`recent`/`get`/`photoIds`). The existing UI is kept and pointed at the new store: the detail dialog at `P/ui/settings/place/PlaceCardHistoryUi.kt:142` with Open Moment (`:157`), Show again and Delete from history.
2. **"Save location with Moments" defaults ON.** It adds no permission and no location request; It is refused inside a zone and while paused (fail closed); It is never in an export, a log, a prompt or Ask.
3. **Live rule:** ±60 s and ≤100 m, held fixes only. The order is zone-stream fix, then the Place Guide fix, then the system last-known. (PLAN had Place Guide first.)
4. **Only a user Forget deletes location rows**, in its own transaction. **Deviation:** PLAN 3's global sweep `... IN (SELECT momentId FROM moment_tombstone)` is DROPPED. The internal `InvalidMomentPurge.kt:69` also writes `moment_tombstone`, so the sweep would break decision 4; Instead, a user Moment Forget deletes the rows of its `requestedSeconds` (WP2).
5. **The import** is a token-paired Developer HTTP route (WP5), not `adb push`.
6. **The backfill** uses an adaptive window chosen by speed class (section 4).
7. **WP7:** the zones map is read-only while offline.
8. **Sequencing deviation:** places.db v3 and every `place_card_log` inventory change move to WP3, so each WP compiles on its own. Until WP3, the old `memory.place.PlaceCardLogEntity` and the new `memory.db.PlaceCardLogEntity` coexist in different packages and different databases.

## 4. Backfill speed classes (`backfill_gps.py`; required for `--emit-import`; the fixed `--window` stays for dry-run comparison)
For a Moment at `t`:
- `prev` and `next` are the media fixes that bracket it;
- `span = next − prev` and `d` is the distance between them;
- `v = d/span`;
- `gapS` is the seconds from `t` to the fix used.

| Class (checked top-down) | Rule | Window (max gap) | accuracyM = min(5000, …) |
|---|---|---|---|
| UNKNOWN | a bracket is missing, or span > 120 min | 10 min | 15 + 1.4·gapS |
| STATIONARY | d ≤ 150 m and v ≤ 0.3 m/s | 60 min | 15 + d |
| WALKING | v ≤ 2.5 m/s | 15 min | 15 + max(v, 0.5)·gapS |
| VEHICLE | v > 2.5 m/s | 5 min | 15 + v·gapS |

- **Method:** INTERPOLATED (linear in time) when both brackets are inside the window. Otherwise NEAREST, when the nearer one is inside it. Otherwise blank.
- **Why these thresholds:** 150 m is about twice the usual EXIF GPS scatter (20-60 m outdoors, worse indoors); 0.3 m/s (about 1 km/h) separates lingering from moving; 2.5 m/s (9 km/h) is above brisk walking (1.3-1.8 m/s) and below an auto or a car; Beyond a 120 min span, an average speed says nothing.
- **Caveat:** there are only about 195 photo GPS points. Most spans are long, many Moments will be UNKNOWN, and a class is a coarse guess, not a measurement. That is why every row keeps `speedClass`, `method` and `fixAgeMs` for later filtering.
- **Dry run:** `summary.md` and stdout give per-class counts of candidates, INTERPOLATED, NEAREST and blank, plus per day. No coordinates.
- **Fixtures:** one case per class; each boundary: exactly 150 m, 0.3 m/s, 2.5 m/s and a 120 min span; each window edge ±1 s; one-sided brackets; the TSV header equals WP5's.
