# Voice marks

A voice mark saves "something worth remembering happened just now" without touching your phone. Say a short command and the app saves a mark for the last few seconds. This page is generated from the same phrase table the app uses, so it always matches what the app understands.

## How to mark

- Tap the glasses while the HUD is visible.
- Or press push-to-talk on the glasses or the phone, then say a command from the lists below.
- Or use the phone notification, the tile or the home button.

There is no hotword and no always-on listening. The app listens only after you press push-to-talk (or after a tap, on the voice path), and stops when you finish speaking.

## What you can say

Use any phrase below. A word like "please", "ok" or "hey" in front is fine. After a mark command you can add what you were doing, for example "mark that, I'm driving". Unknown words save a plain mark. A mark is never lost because the app did not know your word.

Commands: mark a moment, "I missed that" (saves the previous 10 seconds), and undo.

#### Mark a moment
Saves a mark for the last 10 seconds up to the moment you pressed push-to-talk.
- `mark that`
- `mark this`
- `mark it`
- `remember this`
- `remember that`
- `save this`
- `save that`
- `note this`
- `note that`
- `bookmark this`
- `bookmark that`
- `capture this`
- `this is important`
- `that was important`

#### I missed that
Same as a mark: saves the previous 10 seconds, recorded as a voice mark.
- `i missed that`

#### Undo
Deletes the mark from this listen, or your newest voice or tap mark from the last 2 minutes.
- `undo mark`
- `cancel mark`
- `remove mark`

#### Polite starts you can add before any command

- `hey`

## What does not work

- Hindi and other languages are not supported yet. English only for now.
- The word "forget" is never a mark command. It belongs to the Forget feature and is deliberately left out here.
- There is no hotword and no always-on listening.
- Questions phrased as commands are ignored. A sentence that starts with a question word is never a command, so "what did you mark" does nothing. The only exceptions are "can you" and "could you" followed by a command, for example "can you mark that".
- If you say an intent after a negation ("mark that, I'm not driving"), the mark is saved plain, with no intent.

## Privacy

- Audio is never stored or uploaded.
- Recognition runs on the phone's on-device recogniser only.
- Only the time, source and label are kept.

## Turning it off

Voice marks are on by default. To turn them off, open Settings and switch off Voice marks. Each command group (Mark a moment, I missed that, Undo a mark) can also be switched off on its own. Tap and notification marks keep working.

## For developers: keeping this page in sync

The block between the markers is generated by `MarkPhrasesDoc.render()` from `MarkPhrases` (the parser's data table). `VoiceMarksDocTest` fails if a phrase in the table is missing from this page or the page lists a phrase that is not in the table. After changing the table, regenerate with:

`VOICE_MARKS_REGEN=1 ./gradlew --no-daemon :services:voice:testDebugUnitTest --tests '*VoiceMarksDocTest'`

Then run the command again without the variable and commit the result. The README list is generated the same way from `VoiceCommandGroup.examples`.
