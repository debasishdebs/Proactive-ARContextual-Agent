**Not ready for implementation as written.** The deterministic compile gate, current-key binding, saveable drafts, and advisory-only LLM pass follow the rulings. The main gaps concern invariant enforcement and distributed execution. Read-only review; no files changed.

1. **Critical — intent protection conflicts with identity parity and misses indirect throttling.** [I4](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:131) allows intent only through vehicle subtype voting. Existing [ContextFusion](../pca-ctx/int/android-companion/core/model/src/main/kotlin/com/rokid/context/core/model/context/ContextFusion.kt:129) also uses images to latch BT contradiction and exclude REST via WORKOUT.
   **Failure:** BT alone sustains an unrestricted vehicle state; two indoor images contradict BT; fresh stillness then commits REST, capped at two/min. Intent has throttled capture. Strict taint rejects the identity graph; allowing those paths defeats the proof. Define a mandatory non-image capture basis covering temporal state, not merely current reason flags. Document necessary differences from the frozen oracle.

2. **Critical — compilation does not establish a coherent phone/glasses activation.** [§3.4](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:88) puts `phoneGraphHash` in metadata, but current [PlanJson](../pca-s3/int/android-companion/core/capture/src/main/kotlin/com/rokid/context/capture/plan/PlanJson.kt:72) excludes metadata from `configHash`.
   **Failure:** changing only phone inference preserves the hash used for replay/battery qualification despite changing capture behavior. A delayed install or glasses-local trial expiry can also pair one graph with another policy. Bind both executable projections into the configuration identity; specify matching context generations, staged activation, restart recovery, state reset/warm-up, and coordinated rollback.

3. **Important — fixed non-walking precedence violates whole-engine editability.** [§12.1](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:186) fixes FLIGHT > VEHICLE > REST.
   **Failure:** a user cannot prioritize a custom REST rule over vehicle inference even while preserving walking protection and every hard safety bound. Keep walking precedence protected; make the remaining arbitration order graph data.

4. **Important — the raw-forward exemption directly contradicts the binding link rule.** [§3.3](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:82) permits raw forwarding below 1/min and 64 B.
   **Failure:** a small, slow source crosses devices without an aggregate/poll step and still compiles. Remove the exemption. Represent even cheap forwarding through an explicit source-side poll/coalescer, if that operation satisfies the ruling.

5. **Important — the identity contract already contains incompatible bounds/defaults.** [I3](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:130) limits gaps to 60 s, whereas [PlanBounds](../pca-s3/int/android-companion/core/capture/src/main/kotlin/com/rokid/context/capture/plan/PlanBounds.kt:20) allows 300 s and default FLIGHT uses 120/300 s.
   **Failure:** the proposed validator rejects its own identity policy. Also, the graph specifies a 30-minute watch TTL, while production [ContextHub](../pca-ctx/int/android-companion/phone/src/main/java/com/rokid/context/phone/context/ContextHub.kt:31) supplies 25 minutes: watch-only evidence can disagree after minute 25. “Binary64 as today” also needs qualification: current fusion mixes Float and Double.

6. **Important — rate checking conflates evaluation frequency with observation freshness.** The signal table requests phone evaluation every 10 s for GPS sampled every 30 s, while [`E_RATE`](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:99) rejects evaluation above arrival rate.
   **Failure:** legitimate identity paths fail compilation—or implementations quietly reinterpret the rule. Separate acquisition, delivery, and evaluation rates. Specify sample age, coverage, sequence/generation, unavailable values, and restart behavior; otherwise cached or missing summaries can justify restrictions as though fresh.

7. **Important — privacy taint needs an explicit coarse-context declassification boundary.** [I7](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:134) forbids PHONE_ONLY taint crossing links, but permitted `ctx` necessarily depends on GPS/BT.
   **Failure:** transitive taint rejects normal context delivery; clearing taint generically permits a speed comparison or selected-device boolean to escape. Define a narrow, validated `out.ctx` declassifier, including permitted fields/reasons, and keep quantised GPS and BT facts phone-local through replay/export paths.

8. **Important — glasses cost and battery claims lack enforceable resource bounds.** [§5](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:139) treats 96 nodes as roughly 300 cheap operations/s; four windows do not bound their sample counts, memory, or update work.
   **Failure:** a long window exhausts memory despite a small plan. Add total state-byte and weighted-work limits. The 8,192-byte cap is real; the 2.5 KB encoding estimate is plausible but unverified. Remaining below UNKNOWN behavior also does not make camera cost disappear: changing REST/FLIGHT inference can increase camera-open time. Phone-only changes therefore cannot earn unrestricted LIVE solely from four hours of SHADOW.

9. **Important — replay and SHADOW omit observation-dependent uncertainty.** [§8](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-context-rules-dag-design-opus.md:153) recomputes graphs, but candidates can widen GPS demand and change future photo/intent opportunities.
   **Failure:** a candidate needs GPS fixes or images absent from the active engine’s trace; replay invents confidence from unavailable observations. Quantised speed can also alter threshold/pair results. Record sufficient temporal state and mark unsupported counterfactuals uncertain; SHADOW must not silently control real sensing.

**Where my earlier proposal was better:** [§§6, 10, 12, 15–16](.superpowers/codex/context-rules-codex.md:84) specified unavailable-input behavior, evaluator memory/work limits, mandatory non-image comparison, coordinated activation, and replay uncertainty more clearly. Those safeguards should carry over. Its frozen fusion internals do **not** satisfy the broader ruling, and its unchanged-identity claim also needs correction for finding 1.

**Estimate:** 60.5 agent-hours adds correctly, but is an optimistic lower bound. Stateful interpreter parity, temporal taint, two-device recovery, privacy-safe replay, and device validation are compressed substantially. My earlier 53-hour estimate is also weak evidence. A provisional **90–130 incremental agent-hours**, excluding remaining prerequisites and wear time, is a more defensible planning allowance—not a measured estimate.

**48 hours at 100% oracle agreement:** useful supplementary evidence after the proposed goldens/fuzzing, **insufficient as the switching criterion**. Require explicit coverage of expiry boundaries, contradiction latches, reordered arrivals, source revocation/rebuild, clock jumps, reconnects, restarts, and trial rollback. Compare every decision field plus capture and sensing effects. First resolve which oracle behaviors intentionally change to satisfy the invariants; perfect agreement cannot validate an unsafe reference behavior.
